## 1. No Licensed Financial Advice
Finova and its AI services provide automated data organization, calculation, and document retrieval. Finova is **NOT** a certified financial planner, tax advisor, or investment professional. AI outputs do not constitute formal financial, tax, or legal advice.

---

## 2. AI Receipt & Bill OCR Scanning
Finova utilizes multimodal artificial intelligence models (Google Gemini Vision) to parse uploaded receipt images and extract draft transaction fields (merchant name, date, total amount, tax, items, and category).

- **OCR Verification Requirement:** AI extraction is an automated convenience feature and is not guaranteed to be 100% accurate. Image quality, lighting, handwriting, or damaged receipts can affect OCR results.
- **User Responsibility:** You are strictly responsible for reviewing and verifying all extracted figures, merchant titles, and dates before submitting transactions to your ledger.

---

## 3. Financial Document Vault & RAG Search
The Financial Document Vault extracts text from uploaded PDF documents and generates 384-dimensional vector embeddings to answer your questions through Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

- **Grounded Document Answers:** The AI Assistant strictly limits document responses to extracted text context inside uploaded PDFs.
- **Not Official Legal/Contractual Interpretation:** Answers provided by the AI Assistant about loan contracts, tax forms, or insurance policies are simplified text summaries and must not replace official legal or professional review.

---

## 4. Financial Assistant Insights & Calculations
Finova's AI Assistant performs database analytics over your recorded expenses and income. All totals, category spending percentages, and budget comparisons are derived directly from your transaction database using exact mathematical queries. The AI Assistant generates narrative summaries to explain your financial patterns.
